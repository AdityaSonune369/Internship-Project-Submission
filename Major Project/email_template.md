# Phishing Email Template

**Scenario:** Fake IT Support / Password Expiry Notification

---

**Subject:** Action Required: Your Password Expires in 24 Hours

**From:** IT Support Desk <admin@microsoft-security-auth.com>
*(Note: Use a sender address that looks slightly suspicious to train the users to check the sender.)*

**To:** [Victim's Email Address]

**Body:**

Dear User,

This is an automated notification from the IT Security Team.

Your Microsoft 365 account password is set to expire in **24 hours**. To maintain access to your email, files, and other services, please update your password immediately by clicking the secure link below.

[**Keep My Current Password / Update Now**](https://microsoft-account-protection-hub.vercel.app)

If you do not update your password within the next 24 hours, you may be temporarily locked out of your account for security reasons.

Thank you,  
IT Department  
Microsoft 365 Security Team

---
*Disclaimer: This is an automated message. Please do not reply directly to this email.*
